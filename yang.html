Statement

Yang, for me, is about paying attention.

The engineer's job is to make things behave. The artist's job is to notice, and to hold the noticing long enough that someone else can see it too. That's what pulls me toward a camera, a moodboard, a keynote deck — and, honestly, why the Yin side ships better software: because I've spent hours studying how light moves across a face, how a room composes itself, how a story asks to be told. Attention is a transferable skill.

Process

How a Yang project moves through my hands.

01 — Read

Sit with the subject.

Before the mood board, before the shot list — a real conversation. Who is this for, what does it need to say, and what is it not?

02 — Frame

Sketch the shape.

Rough moodboards, thumbnail comps, a rhythm. The visual system before any single visual.

03 — Make

Shoot, design, iterate.

Camera in hand, cursor in Figma, deck in Keynote — whatever the medium calls for. Kaizen: small, considered passes.

04 — Ship

Deliver something usable.

Not just a folder of assets — a system the team can carry forward. Captions, alt text, file naming, the boring parts done well.

Web · Rebrand · Counseling practice

Rapha's Counseling Center

Ann-Marie Blaise's online counseling practice had outgrown its Wix template. I rebuilt it as a calm, nine-page site — a green, rooted identity, her own portrait front and centre, and booking that takes about two minutes.

  • Google Calendar booking with video links for all six services, with a WhatsApp fallback
  • Shows Trinidad time next to the visitor's own, for clients across the Caribbean and the Netherlands
  • New FAQ, privacy, accessibility, terms and refund pages
  • Every old Wix URL redirected, so existing links and search ranking carry over
  • Light & dark mode, WebP photography, structured data for search — deployed on Vercel

Web · Rebrand · Event venue & Airbnb

G.E.M.S De La Rosa

A garden event venue and Airbnb stay in Trinidad whose Wix site opened on stock beach photos rather than the venue itself. The rebuild puts the venue first — the garden from above, clear prices, and the owner's Trini voice kept in every menu item.

  • Two ways in: book the event space or stay at the Airbnb
  • Package pricing in TTD with USD estimates, driven by one editable price list
  • Event booking form, photo gallery with lightbox, and a guide to things to do nearby
  • WhatsApp, directions and a prefilled contact form, one tap away on mobile
  • Old Wix links redirected, structured data for search — deployed on Vercel

Growing up on the water changes what "beautiful" looks like. Warm palettes, generous white space, unhurried compositions, the sense that a good frame should feel like a Sunday afternoon. Even when the subject is Atlanta, the light is still coming from the Caribbean.
